What Is a Compound Statement?

Compound statement: two simple statements joined by the word "and" or the word "or."

Statement A
AND
OR
Statement B
  • • Each part can be true or false on its own
  • • The joining word decides when the WHOLE statement is true
Logic Practice

Logic in Everyday Life

Before using math, let's see how we use "AND" and "OR" in daily statements.

"It is raining AND I have an umbrella."

For this whole statement to be TRUE, what must happen?

"I will walk OR I will take the bus."

For this statement to be TRUE, what must happen?

Excellent!

You understand the basic logic. In mathematics, it works exactly the same way.

AND

Overlap & Intersection

An inequality with AND requires both parts to be true. On the 3-level number line, the solution is where the lines overlap.

$$x > 1 \quad \text{AND} \quad x < 4$$

Written together: $$1 < x < 4$$

Interval Notation: $$(1, 4)$$

OR

Union of Solutions

An inequality with OR requires at least one part to be true. On the number line, we keep everything that is shaded by either inequality.

$$x \le -1 \quad \text{OR} \quad x > 3$$

Interval Notation: $$(-\infty, -1] \cup (3, \infty)$$

Step-by-Step

Solving Examples

Read the algebraic solution, then build the graph to verify it.

Example 1: "AND" statement

$$-6 \le 4x - 2 < 10$$

Part A

$$ -6 \le 4x - 2 $$

$$ -4 \le 4x $$ (add 2)

$$ x \ge -1 $$

Part B

$$ 4x - 2 < 10 $$

$$ 4x < 12 $$ (add 2)

$$ x < 3 $$

Now build the graph for x ≥ -1 AND x < 3

Example 2: "OR" statement

$$3x - 8 \le -14 \quad \text{OR} \quad 5x + 7 > 17$$

Left Side

$$ 3x - 8 \le -14 $$

$$ x \le -2 $$ (+8, then /3)

Right Side

$$ 5x + 7 > 17 $$

$$ x > 2 $$ (-7, then /5)

Now build the graph for x ≤ -2 OR x > 2
